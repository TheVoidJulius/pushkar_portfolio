This repo is my corner of the internet. It isn't a template with my name swapped in: it's a small world you scroll through, and it has a resident.

## Meet Mikuro 🐱

Mikuro is a liquid-chrome cat who lives on the site. She has opinions about everything on it.

- **She's asleep when you arrive.** A little terminal boots up; press Enter and a swarm of chrome droplets pools together into her. She yawns, grumbles "five more minutes", and then pops awake.
- **She gives you the tour.** As you scroll, she floats beside each section and talks you through it, in her own (pre-recorded) voice.
- **She has a life.** She drinks coffee, gets distracted, flinches when you flip the lights, and goes quiet when you put music on.
- **She has a house.** Scroll all the way down and she walks through the snow to her neon cyber-house and goes to bed. There's a sign on the door. You can choose to respect it.

## Made with

Next.js, React, Three.js and a lot of hand-written shaders. Mikuro is built entirely from code, with no 3D model files. Her voice comes from ElevenLabs, recorded once ahead of time.

<details>
<summary><b>Running it locally</b></summary>

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:3000
```

It works out of the box with the bundled content in `supabase/seed-content.json`. Optional extras, set in `.env.local` (see `.env.example`):

- **Supabase**, to edit everything from the `/admin` dashboard instead of the JSON file.
- **ElevenLabs**, to re-record Mikuro's lines after changing them: `npm run voice:bake -- --yes`.

The owner's guide to the dashboard is in [docs/CLIENT-GUIDE.md](docs/CLIENT-GUIDE.md).

</details>
